# Sign poses

Each `<GLOSS>.json` holds 2D body, hand and face points tracked with MediaPipe
from that sign's reference video on [ASL Signbank](https://aslsignbank.com)
(Hochgesang, J. A., Crasborn, O., & Lillo-Martin, D. (2017–2026). ASL Signbank.
https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.9741788).

As an adaptation of those videos, these files are licensed under
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
Made with `scripts/extract_poses.py`.
